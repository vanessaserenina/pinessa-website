About Pinessa

Supplying Excellence to Indonesian Hospitality

Pinessa was founded in 2007 with a straightforward goal: to give Indonesian hotels access to hotel-grade amenities that match international standards — at supply prices that make business sense.

Over more than 15 years, we have grown from a small regional supplier into a trusted name across Java, Bali, Sumatra, and beyond. Our team understands the demands of hotel operations — consistency, reliability, and the importance of every detail a guest encounters.
